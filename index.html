Four programmes, one pathway from GNM to M.Sc

Each course is affiliated to RGUHS and recognised by the Indian Nursing Council.

Master of Nursing

Advanced study in clinical specialities, research and nursing leadership.

Post Basic Degree in Nursing

A degree route for GNM-qualified nurses, awarded by RGUHS.

Bachelor of Nursing

Four years of training for care in hospitals, community and day-care settings.

General Nursing and Midwifery

Practical training to work alongside doctors and specialist teams.
